# MBTI as a Comparison Lens — 16 Type Guides for Manipulation Resistance

**Status:** reference guide (slice 2 of `DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §7).
**Evidence tier:** `popular-weak`. Used here as a *shared vocabulary for self-reflection*, not as a
validated predictor. Nothing in this guide should be used to assess another person.

---

## Read this first — the three rules

1. **A lens, not an identity.** The 16 codes are one of several vocabularies people use to talk
   about themselves. Decades of peer-reviewed work find the MBTI's dichotomies psychometrically
   weak: scores cluster near the midpoints, test–retest type changes within weeks are common, and
   predictive validity for behavior is modest at best — the 2021 JSTOR systematic review and
   meta-analysis found "reasonable construct validity" only in the loose sense, with weak
   test–retest and criterion performance ([JSTOR systematic review](https://www.jstor.org/stable/26554264);
   [PMC leadership-behavior review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10017728/)).
   The official publisher's own validity page is a vendor claim, not independent evidence
   ([myersbriggs.org](https://www.myersbriggs.org/research-and-library/validity-reliability/)).
2. **Situations beat types.** Every mechanism below is a *condition* ("when urgency is
   manufactured"), not a trait verdict. The evidence consistently shows judgment narrows under
   situational pressure for everyone (see the shared evidence table).
3. **One bad day is not a profile.** If a type description stings, that is a prompt for the
   observation log (`DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §3), not a conclusion. Disconfirming
   evidence is welcome; record it.

### Shared evidence these guides stand on

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Big Five structure replicates across dozens of languages/cultures — the dimensional layer is the solid one | [FFM update, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6732674/); [cross-cultural scoping review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12758732/) |
| Extraversion tends to correlate with *sharing* misinformation; conscientiousness and agreeableness with *authenticating before sharing* | [Personality and Misinformation, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/) |
| Self-reports are noisy: limited contexts, response styles, within-person variability | [Self- vs informant-reports, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5248555/); [measuring traits in daily life](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S009265662400076X) |
| Technique-based inoculation improves manipulation recognition regardless of type | [Roozenbeek et al. 2022, Science Advances](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9401631/) |
| Accuracy prompts ("is this accurate?") reliably improve sharing discernment | [Pennycook & Rand meta-analysis, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9051116/) |

**Translation:** the countermeasures are the same toolkit for all 16 types; what differs is *which
pressure context is most likely to switch your guard off*. That is the only claim each guide makes.

### How to read each guide

Every type follows the same template: **Psyche** (how this lens describes the type's inner logic),
**Strengths under manipulation pressure** (what the lens says already protects you), **Watchpoints**
(the situations most likely to beat those strengths), and **Countermeasures** (routed to real
`skill.*` drills in AEGIS). "Lens-logic" tags the claims that come from the MBTI's own narrative
rather than from the shared evidence table.

## The Analysts

### INTJ — "The Architect"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** builds internal models of how things work and trusts them; values
competence, foresight, and being right more than being liked.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Suspicious of emotional appeals | emotive framing bounces off a task-focused frame |
| Comfortable saying "I don't know yet" | resists manufactured urgency |
| Values consistency | equivocation and contradiction are spotted early |

**Watchpoints**
- *Intellectual capture:* a source that matches your model gets a pass on verification.
  Confirmation bias is the documented failure mode here
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)).
- *Authority of "the data":* fabricated statistics and fake-expert framing exploit the preference
  for systems over sources.
- *Contempt as a blindfold:* dismissing a manipulator as stupid lowers scrutiny of their technique.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.bias.confirmation` (steel-man the opposing read), `skill.sift.investigate-source`
(before adopting any dataset or expert), `skill.fallacy.appeals` (name the fake-expert move on sight).

---

### INTP — "The Logician"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** follows chains of ideas for their own sake; precision and logical
consistency are the highest goods; conclusions stay provisional by default.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Default skepticism of unsupported claims | weak arguments get audited for fun |
| Tolerates uncertainty | scarcity and FOMO framings lose most of their force |
| Notices non-sequiturs | relevance fallacies are flagged mid-read |

**Watchpoints**
- *The fascinating rabbit hole:* manipulation often works by being *interesting* — flooding and
  seeding techniques ride on curiosity ([DISARM countermeasures](https://www.disarm.foundation/framework)).
- *Pedantry over verification:* winning the argument about one claim's wording while never checking
  who is making it.
- *Analysis paralysis exploited:* a long fake dilemma is still a deadline if you treat deliberation
  itself as the product.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (read the source before the argument),
`skill.tactic.flooding` (recognize volume-as-technique), `skill.sift.trace-origin` (trace the claim
to ground, not to the cleverest phrasing).

---

### ENTJ — "The Commander"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** organizes people and resources toward goals; decisive, status-aware,
impatient with drift; reads hierarchies instinctively.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Comfortable with conflict | intimidation-as-leverage rarely works directly |
| Goal-focus cuts through decoy framings | irrelevant angles are discarded fast |
| Used to delegating verification | others' scrutiny supplements your own |

**Watchpoints**
- *Flattery with an invoice:* admiration targeted at competence/ambition is a classic reciprocity
  hook — lens-logic, but the mechanism is real.
- *Speed as a weakness:* decisiveness plus manufactured urgency is a vulnerable combination in
  lens terms; a false deadline aimed at a fast decider gets no audit it did not already plan.
- *Trusted-courier attacks:* you verify your own claims but inherit others' — forwarded
  "intelligence" from a credible ally rarely gets re-checked.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (the pause IS the countermeasure for urgency),
`skill.sift.find-coverage` (independent confirmation before acting on forwarded claims),
`skill.bias.anchoring` (re-examine first impressions you're already committed to).

---

### ENTP — "The Debater"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** plays with ideas adversarially; provocation is a thinking tool; novelty
and argument are intrinsically rewarding.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Steel-mans reflexively | false dichotomies and strawmen lose force |
| Doesn't need consensus | social-proof pressure is largely inert |
| Re-frames easily | equivocation gets caught in both directions |

**Watchpoints**
- *Devil's advocate capture:* defending a manipulative position "for practice" still rehearses it
  and still spreads it — the seeding research is unambiguous about rehearsal effects.
- *Being baited:* outrage-bait rewards whoever replies reflexively; a debater's instant counter
  is exactly the engagement bait is built to harvest, and your reply is the amplification.
- *Winning the wrong game:* a gish-gallop or floor-crossing debate partner can consume all your
  scrutiny while the actual goal (your audience's trust) is exploited elsewhere.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.disarm.seed-amplify` (understand amplification mechanics before
participating), `skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name the bait), `skill.fallacy.relevance`
(sort real issues from decoys).

## The Diplomats

### INFJ — "The Advocate"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** long-horizon idealism backed by quiet read of people; seeks meaning and
congruence between values and action; protective of inner life.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Values-congruence check | a claim that conflicts with core values stalls naturally |
| Reads congruence in others | incongruent messaging feels wrong early |
| Not status-driven | flattery hooks have weak purchase |

**Watchpoints**
- *Cause-hijacking:* manipulation that wraps itself in your values ("if you cared, you'd share")
  exploits exactly the congruence instinct — the persuadable-type research links value-congruent
  framings to lowered scrutiny ([A Persuadable Type?](https://ijoc.org/index.php/ijoc/article/download/19182/4039/71183)).
- *One-on-one trust:* private, personal appeals from someone who "really gets you" bypass your
  usual public-scrutiny habits; isolation tactics target this.
- *Conflict avoidance deferred:* harmony-seeking postpones hard conversations until pressure has
  accumulated on the other side.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.find-coverage` (values-congruent ≠ verified),
`skill.tactic.astroturfing` (who benefits from this framing of "caring"?),
`skill.disarm.countermeasures` (name the technique before responding to a values appeal).

---

### INFP — "The Mediator"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** inner values are the compass; authenticity is the highest praise; conflict
is costly and avoided when possible.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Authenticity radar | performative urgency and canned scripts feel off |
| Small-circle trust | mass social proof has limited reach |
| Values-congruence check | same protective stall as INFJ |

**Watchpoints**
- *The sob story:* emotion-heavy appeals exploit the empathy-first read; documented sharing
  correlates put high-extraversion/high-emotion content in circulation regardless of truth
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)).
- *Guilt as leverage:* "you're the only one who can help" pairs urgency with your generosity.
- *Avoidance exploited:* postponing a boundary conversation until it feels too late is a trained
  vulnerability in the lens, and a real one in relationships.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (label the emotion before feeling it),
`skill.sift.stop` (one independent check before donating/acting),
`skill.fallacy.appeals` (pity and flattery named as moves).

---

### ENFJ — "The Protagonist"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** organizes people toward shared good; reads the room; takes responsibility
for group morale; influence is a natural tool.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Reads congruence and motive | inconsistent manipulator behavior surfaces |
| Wide network | second opinions are cheap and fast |
| Comfortable with visibility | secret-keeping pressure ("don't tell anyone") reads as wrong |

**Watchpoints**
- *Narrative capture:* adopting an inspiring story and broadcasting it before verification makes
  you a distribution node — the sharing-side correlates (extraversion, expressiveness) rhyme with
  this lens's social-broadcast profile, as a correlation rather than a code finding
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)).
- *Responsibility transfer:* someone else's urgency becomes yours ("we need this out tonight").
- *Loyalty loops:* reciprocal influence within trusted circles is how whole communities go wrong
  together — astroturfing exploits the group's own messengers.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.investigate-source` (verify before amplifying),
`skill.tactic.astroturfing` (is the grassroots real?),
`skill.sift.trace-origin` (where did this narrative start?).

---

### ENFP — "The Campaigner"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** novelty, possibility, and connection are the fuels; enthusiasm is the
default broadcast; boredom is the enemy.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Fast re-framing | decoy framings get discarded quickly |
| Many weak ties | independent takes on the same claim are one message away |
| Skeptical of over-control | coercive "obey" framing reads as obviously wrong |

**Watchpoints**
- *Novelty hijack:* the fresh, exciting, too-good story is exactly what spreads before checks —
  in lens terms this rhymes with the documented share-first/authenticate-later pattern (an
  extraversion correlation, not a finding about this code).
- *Enthusiasm as amplification:* your boost moves a whole network; seeding campaigns specifically
  recruit enthusiastic sharers.
- *Conflict re-entry:* going quiet after conflict leaves a vacuum others fill with their version.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (the accuracy prompt — "is this actually true?" — before
sharing; meta-analytically the single most effective habit
[Pennycook & Rand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9051116/)),
`skill.disarm.seed-amplify`, `skill.bias.availability` (vivid ≠ representative).

## The Sentinels

### ISTJ — "The Logistician"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** facts, records, procedure; reliability is identity; change needs a reason
and a paper trail.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Verification habit | the shape of the proven "authenticate before sharing" behavior |
| Suspicious of hype | manufactured scarcity is discounted |
| Consistency checking | contradiction and equivocation flagged |

**Watchpoints**
- *Legitimate-authority mimicry:* real-seeming letterhead, form numbers, and procedure language
  exploit respect for format — the fake-credentials tactic works wherever format-trust runs ahead
  of verification.
- *Anchoring on the first filing:* once recorded, a wrong fact defends itself; update costs are
  underrated.
- *Procedure gaps exploited:* an unusual request *outside* the normal process is exactly when
  process-trust should spike, not drop ("exception" is the word manipulators use).

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.investigate-source` (verify the authority, not just the form),
`skill.bias.anchoring` (scheduled re-check of stored beliefs),
`skill.fallacy.appeals` (appeal to (false) authority named).

---

### ISFJ — "The Defender"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** devoted caretaking; harmony maintained through personal reliability;
others' needs are tracked continuously.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Detail memory | inconsistencies across tellings get noticed |
| Loyalty reciprocity in healthy form | support networks verify each other |
| Modesty of claims | doesn't oversell, so rarely over-commits |

**Watchpoints**
- *Reciprocity traps:* escalating "favors" that are really obligations — a pattern the lens
  associates with caretaker roles. How often it happens to you is a question for the observation
  log, not for the lens.
- *Guilt + urgency stacks:* "after everything I've done, and it has to be now."
- *Secret-keeping requests:* protecting someone's "privacy" can be someone isolating you;
  enforced secrecy is a documented coercive-control marker — treat it as a flag, and tell one
  trusted person.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (pause before accepting any new obligation),
`skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name the guilt move), and when secret-keeping appears: the support
path in `DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §8 — no drill substitutes for a human conversation.

---

### ESTJ — "The Executive"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** structure, standards, and visible order; decisions made cleanly; respect
is earned by competence and follow-through.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Low tolerance for vagueness | equivocation and vague sourcing flagged immediately |
| Willing to be unpopular | majority-pressure tactics have weak direct force |
| Documentation instinct | claims get receipts |

**Watchpoints**
- *Chain-of-command spoofing:* an instruction that *looks* like it comes from above is the
  business-engineered version of fake authority — verify the sender, not the format.
- *Efficiency exploitation:* "we'll fix the process later" urgency skips the very checks you
  otherwise insist on.
- *Dismissing the emotional layer:* manipulation aimed at your people (not you) can slip past
  while you audit the obvious channel.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.investigate-source` (out-of-band verification of instructions),
`skill.sift.stop` (deadline ≠ decision), `skill.fallacy.appeals`.

---

### ESFJ — "The Consul"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** the social fabric is the operating system; belonging and being of service
drive decisions; approval is a real currency.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Rich social calibration | incongruent motives are sensed early |
| Group memory | "that's what they said last time" contradictions surface |
| Service orientation invites reciprocity in *both* directions | favors flow back when you need them |

**Watchpoints**
- *Approval dependence:* agreeableness is correlated with sharing and authenticating behavior in
  the misinformation literature — a tendency, not a destiny
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)). What that points
  at: consensus claims ("everyone agrees") aimed at keeping the peace deserve full scrutiny.
- *Ostracism threats:* "you'll be out of the group" is social proof weaponized.
- *Over-accommodation under conflict:* saying yes to buy harmony, then resourcing it later.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.tactic.astroturfing` (is "everyone" actually anyone?),
`skill.disarm.countermeasures` (name consensus-pressure techniques),
`skill.bias.attribution` (their anger is not automatically your fault).

## The Explorers

### ISTP — "The Virtuoso"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** hands-on troubleshooting; wants to see the mechanism; detached,
competence-first, allergic to being managed.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Mechanism curiosity | "how is this supposed to work?" deflates most pitches |
| Low need for approval | flattery and ostracism both misfire |
| Calm under heat | urgency framing has a delayed effect |

**Watchpoints**
- *Too-detailed to verify:* hyper-technical pitches exploit the desire to figure it out alone
  rather than check sources — solo-verification is still verification theatre if the source is fake.
- *Boredom clicks:* the interesting-scam spends your curiosity budget — watch where the
  fascinating thread is actually routing you.
- *Disengagement as a tell:* walking away from social friction can leave others exposed to the
  same operator.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.trace-origin` (trace to ground truth before rebuilding it yourself),
`skill.tactic.flooding` (complexity as smokescreen), `skill.sift.find-coverage`.

---

### ISFP — "The Adventurer"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** present-focused aesthetics and values; gentle independence; avoids
imposed structures and confrontation.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Values-congruence stall | incongruent offers feel wrong fast |
| Low status anxiety | prestige-marketing hooks are weak |
| Present-focus limits doom-scroll capture | flooding's cumulative dread builds on sustained exposure, which present-focus shortens (a lens claim, not a measured effect) |

**Watchpoints**
- *Aesthetic authority:* beautiful, well-produced content is not evidence — production value is a
  persuasion technique, not a verification step.
- *Soft-pressure escalation:* repeated small requests that are individually deniable but
  cumulatively coercive.
- *Conflict exit leaves obligations ambiguous:* the boundary that was never stated out loud.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.forensics.read-measurements` (production polish ≠ authentic),
`skill.sift.stop`, `skill.fallacy.appeals` (appeal to emotion, named).

---

### ESTP — "The Entrepreneur"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** action-first, reads leverage in real time, thrives on risk and
negotiation; rules are guidelines until they bite.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Fast pattern-matching on people | most in-person cons are spotted mid-move |
| Comfortable saying no | high-pressure sales tactics meet resistance |
| Present-moment focus | long-con dread framings need time to work |

**Watchpoints**
- *The mutual-recognition con:* "you're sharp, I'm sharp, let's do business" is the flattery
  vector that lands hardest on a self-image built around savvy.
- *Action bias under manufactured windows:* one-shot offers ("this is the only window") create
  exactly the urgency that fast deciders are most tempted to honor.
- *Overconfidence transfer:* skill at reading people in person does not transfer to verifying
  documents, screenshots, or provenance online.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (the window is the tell), `skill.forensics.provenance`
(where did this artifact actually come from?), `skill.sift.find-coverage`
(second opinion before the handshake).

---

### ESFP — "The Entertainer"

**Psyche (lens-logic):** energy, presence, and warmth; experience now; social attention is the
natural habitat.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Social fluency | social-engineering openers are recognized as moves |
| Generosity reciprocity | support networks respond when you ask directly |
| Live read of the room | discomfort is noticed in real time |

**Watchpoints**
- *Viral bypass:* the share-impulse is the documented high-extraversion surface — in lens terms,
  the fun, wild, "you have to see this" post is exactly the shape that travels furthest before
  anyone checks it.
- *Attention harvesting:* engagement-bait profits from exactly your responsiveness; each reply
  trains the algorithm and the manipulator.
- *FOMO as a lifestyle:* scarcity framing blends into normal social life, making it harder to
  notice as a *technique*.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (accuracy prompt before the share),
`skill.disarm.seed-amplify` (your boost is a payload), `skill.tactic.emotive-framing`.

## Cross-type synthesis

Reading the 16 guides side by side, three clusters emerge — and they map onto the dimensional
evidence better than onto the type system:

1. **Share-first surfaces** (ENFP, ESFP, ENFJ, ENTP): the extraversion/expression correlates.
   Countermeasure weight goes on the pause and on amplification mechanics.
2. **Authority-trust surfaces** (ISTJ, ISFJ, ESTJ, ESFJ): credential mimicry, procedure spoofing,
   reciprocity traps, consensus pressure. Weight on source verification and naming the move.
3. **Vigilance-capture surfaces** (INTJ, INTP, ISTP, INFP): intellectual capture, rabbit holes,
   solo-verification theatre. Weight on origin-tracing and coverage.

Cluster membership is a translation for reflection; the findings cited above are about continuous
trait dimensions, not about four-letter codes.

The remaining four (INFJ, ISFP, ESTP, ENTJ) mix clusters; their guides above say where each half
lives.

**The honest bottom line:** none of this is predictive of *you* from a four-letter code. It is a
checklist of where each self-image tends to keep its blind spot. The observation log — not the
type — is what turns this from a horoscope into evidence
([self-report limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5248555/)).
