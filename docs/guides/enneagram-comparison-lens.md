# Enneagram as a Comparison Lens — 9 Type Guides for Manipulation Resistance

**Status:** reference guide (slice 2 of `DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §7).
**Evidence tier:** `popular-weak`. Used as a *motivational vocabulary for self-reflection* — the
Enneagram organizes types around core motivation rather than behavior, a useful counterweight to
the MBTI's habit-descriptions. It is not a validated predictor, and nothing here is a tool for
assessing another person.

---

## Read this first — the three rules

1. **A lens, not an identity.** The most comprehensive empirical treatment — Hook et al. (2020),
   *Journal of Clinical Psychology*, a systematic review of 104 independent samples — found **mixed
   evidence of reliability and validity**
   ([PubMed 33332604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33332604/)). Construct validity is where the
   literature is thinnest; no meta-analysis of criterion validity exists. Test–retest estimates
   vary widely by instrument (roughly .50–.84); for comparison, well-validated Big Five
   instruments sit at .80–.90 (secondary summary:
   [peer-reviewed evidence review](https://www.cogn-iq.org/blog/enneagram-validity/)).
2. **Situations beat types.** Every mechanism below is a *condition* under which judgment narrows,
   not a trait verdict. Notably, the 2020 review's cited work found 87% of people could predict
   their type from descriptions alone — a classic Barnum-effect signal. That is why every guide
   below names what would **disconfirm** it in your observation log
   ([Wikipedia summary of the review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enneagram_of_Personality)).
3. **One bad day is not a profile.** A description that stings is a prompt for the observation log
   (`DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §3), not a conclusion. Record disconfirming examples.

### Shared evidence these guides stand on

Same evidence base as the MBTI guide — see
[mbti-comparison-lens.md](mbti-comparison-lens.md#shared-evidence-these-guides-stand-on) for the
full table and links. In summary:

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| The dimensional layer (Big Five) is the solid one; Enneagram types overlap substantially with Big Five dimensions | [FFM update, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6732674/); [Hook et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33332604/) |
| Judgment narrows under situational pressure for everyone; technique-recognition training helps regardless of type | [Roozenbeek et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9401631/) |
| Accuracy prompts ("is this accurate?") improve sharing discernment | [Pennycook & Rand meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9051116/) |
| Self-report is noisy; observation beats introspection alone | [Self- vs informant-reports](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5248555/) |

**Translation:** the countermeasures are the same toolkit for all 9 types; what differs is *which
core motivation, once engaged, switches your guard off*. Because the Enneagram is motivation-first,
its watchpoints describe *what you want in the moment* rather than how you habitually behave —
that is the lens's own claim, and it is why the watchpoints read like lures rather than habits.

### How to read each guide

Same template as the MBTI guide: **Psyche** (the type's claimed core motivation — lens-logic by
definition), **Strengths under manipulation pressure**, **Watchpoints** (the motivational hooks
most likely to beat those strengths), **Countermeasures** (routed to real `skill.*` drills in
AEGIS), and one *disconfirming observation* the log can use to test the lens's fit for you.

## The Gut Types (8/9/1) — motivation: control, peace, integrity through autonomy

### Type 8 — The Challenger

**Psyche (lens-logic):** control through directness; vulnerability is guarded; injustice triggers
confrontation.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Comfortable with conflict | intimidation rarely works directly |
| Reads power dynamics | leverage plays are seen mid-move |
| Natural boundary-setting | isolation tactics meet an explicit "no" |

**Watchpoints**
- *The mutual-recognition con:* "you respect strength, I show strength" — flattery aimed at the
  control drive; the operator matches your frame, then steers it.
- *Justice baiting:* injustice framings trigger confrontation and amplify the baiter's reach —
  your response is the amplification
  ([seed/amplify mechanics](https://www.disarm.foundation/framework)).
- *Vulnerability cost:* the guardedness that protects also delays asking for help; isolation
  tactics exploit exactly that delay.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.disarm.seed-amplify` (what does your confrontation serve?),
`skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name the injustice bait), `skill.disarm.countermeasures`
(pre-decide the response instead of debating in the moment). For the vulnerability delay: name a
trusted person *in advance*, not in crisis.

*Disconfirming observation:* a confrontation you chose *without* an injustice frame doing the
choosing.

### Type 9 — The Peacemaker

**Psyche (lens-logic):** peace through accommodation; conflict is expensive; others' agendas get
adopted by default when assertion costs more than compliance.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Broad perspective | single-source capture is harder |
| De-escalation skill | aggression-as-leverage misfires |
| Patient verification | urgency framings decay on a patient timeline |

**Watchpoints**
- *Agenda adoption:* the lens's direct hook — someone else's urgent agenda becomes yours because
  assertion costs more than compliance.
- *Numbing loops:* comfort rituals (scrolling, streaming) as conflict-avoidance; flooding works
  by exhausting discernment, and a numbed state amplifies that
  ([DISARM flooding](https://www.disarm.foundation/framework)).
- *Ambiguous boundaries:* the boundary never stated out loud stays negotiable — and negotiable
  boundaries are found, not imposed.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (assert the pause — it is the cheapest form of assertion),
`skill.tactic.astroturfing` (whose agenda is this, really?),
`skill.bias.attribution` (their urgency is not automatically your task).

*Disconfirming observation:* a boundary you asserted *early*, without conflict first.

### Type 1 — The Reformer

**Psyche (lens-logic):** an internal critic measuring the gap between how things are and how they
should be; improvement is the standing task; errors are personally loud.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Error-detection reflex | fabricated inconsistencies get caught |
| Principle-first reasoning | "everyone does it" framings stall |
| Self-policing | you audit yourself harder than most manipulators would |

**Watchpoints**
- *Moral licensing via correctness:* "I checked, so I can share" — one early verification becomes
  a license for later, unverified amplification. The accuracy-prompt research implies the prompt
  must be *repeated per share*, not paid once
  ([Pennycook & Rand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9051116/)).
- *Indignation as a lever:* "this is an outrage and people need to know" pairs your correction
  instinct with urgency — you become the amplifier with a clean conscience.
- *System-capture:* a persuasive systemic story can absorb months of energy; flooding benefits
  from exactly this absorption.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (re-run the accuracy prompt per share, not per week),
`skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name "outrage" as a technique),
`skill.disarm.seed-amplify` (what does your amplification serve?).

*Disconfirming observation:* a strong, checked claim you amplified and *don't* feel licensed about.

## The Heart Types (2/3/4) — motivation: connection, value, significance through relationship

### Type 2 — The Helper

**Psyche (lens-logic):** worth is earned through being needed; giving is the default; own needs
are tracked last, if at all.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Read of others' states | incongruent helpers are noticed |
| Relationship density | outside perspectives are one call away |
| Genuine generosity | real reciprocity networks beat fake ones |

**Watchpoints**
- *Need-creation:* manipulators manufacture a need, then let you fill it — the lens's most direct
  hook. Guilt+urgency stacks ("only you can help, now") exploit the worth-through-helping logic.
- *Reciprocity inversion:* escalating "favors" that quietly become obligations.
- *Boundary debt:* deferred boundary-setting compounds into resentment, which itself gets
  leveraged ("after all I've done…").

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (pause before accepting any new obligation),
`skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (label the guilt/urgency stack), and for secret-keeping requests,
the support path in `DESIGN-personal-defense-profile.md` §8 — enforced secrecy is a
coercive-control marker, not a favor.

*Disconfirming observation:* help you gave *without* checking whether the need was manufactured.

### Type 3 — The Achiever

**Psyche (lens-logic):** value is demonstrated through results; image and competence are the
products; failure is managed, not shown.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Results-focus cuts through decoys | irrelevant framings discarded fast |
| Wide competence network | second opinions are fast and high-quality |
| Image-consistency check | performative manipulation reads as off-key |

**Watchpoints**
- *Prestige-marketing:* opportunities, credentials, and "visibility" that flatter the achievement
  drive — flattery-with-an-invoice, aimed at this type's core currency.
- *Speed as a weakness:* decisiveness plus manufactured urgency is a vulnerable combination in
  lens terms; the dimensional research links conscientiousness and extraversion to sharing and
  authenticating behavior, which says nothing about this type directly
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)). What it points at:
  a false deadline aimed at a fast decider gets no audit it did not already plan.
- *Consistency capture:* defending a prior public position to stay consistent keeps the wrong
  claim alive to protect the image.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (deadline ≠ decision), `skill.bias.anchoring`
(re-examine first commitments), `skill.sift.find-coverage` (verify forwarded "opportunities"
before acting on them).

*Disconfirming observation:* a position you reversed publicly *without* the image cost feeling
fatal.

### Type 4 — The Individualist

**Psyche (lens-logic):** identity through depth and uniqueness; authentic feeling is the compass;
the missing thing gets the attention.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Authenticity radar | performative scripts feel off |
| Depth tolerance | shallow hype framings decay |
| Values-congruence stall | incongruent offers feel wrong fast |

**Watchpoints**
- *The understood-by-one con:* "they finally *get* me" — targeted recognition exploits the longing
  to be seen; targeted recognition is a documented social-engineering opener.
- *Envy-frame stacking:* comparison framings ("others already have this") pair scarcity with
  identity — scarcity plus identity is a powerful stack.
- *Dramatization capture:* emotionally vivid narratives get less scrutiny; vividness is the
  sharing correlate, not a verification signal
  ([misinformation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193381/)).

**Countermeasures** → `skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name the recognition hook),
`skill.bias.availability` (vivid ≠ representative),
`skill.sift.stop` (one independent check before acting on a personal appeal).

*Disconfirming observation:* feeling understood by a group *without* your scrutiny dropping.

## The Head Types (5/6/7) — motivation: competence, security, satisfaction through thinking

### Type 5 — The Investigator

**Psyche (lens-logic):** competence through understanding; resources (energy, attention, privacy)
are conserved; the world is best met from a well-stocked mind.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Default skepticism | unsupported claims get audited for fun |
| Tolerates uncertainty | scarcity/FOMO framings lose force |
| Notices non-sequiturs | relevance fallacies flagged mid-read |

**Watchpoints**
- *The fascinating rabbit hole:* on curious minds, being *interesting* is often the whole con —
  flooding and seeding ride curiosity ([DISARM](https://www.disarm.foundation/framework)).
- *Pedantry over verification:* winning the wording argument while never checking the source.
- *Pre-digestion hook:* "let me simplify this for you" exploits the energy budget; pre-digested
  claims are the manipulation surface here.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.stop` (read the source before the argument),
`skill.tactic.flooding` (volume-as-technique), `skill.sift.trace-origin`.

*Disconfirming observation:* a fascinating topic where you *still* checked sources first.

### Type 6 — The Loyalist

**Psyche (lens-logic):** security through vigilance; worst-case rehearsal is a thinking style;
loyalty is the currency of trust.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Vigilant anomaly detection | spoofed authority and fake urgency get flagged |
| Contingency thinking | "what's the catch?" is a standing habit |
| Loyal networks verify each other | group verification supplements your own |

**Watchpoints**
- *Counter-phobic approach:* running *toward* the threat to prove it harmless — the lens's most
  dangerous hook, because it defeats your own vigilance deliberately.
- *Authority ambivalence:* trusting and doubting authority simultaneously; manipulators exploit
  whichever half is louder right now — deferring to credentials, or distrusting the *wrong*
  things (the anti-establishment con).
- *Reassurance loops:* seeking certainty from others is itself a social-engineering surface; a
  confident liar resolves doubt better than a nervous truth-teller.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.sift.investigate-source` (verify the authority, don't oscillate),
`skill.tactic.false-balance` (false balance is not vigilance),
`skill.sift.find-coverage` (independent confirmation, not reassurance).

*Disconfirming observation:* a threat you approached carefully *without* needing reassurance.

### Type 7 — The Enthusiast

**Psyche (lens-logic):** satisfaction through optionality and stimulation; pain is planned around;
the next interesting thing is always available.

| Strengths under pressure | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Optionality instinct | one-shot offers read as suspicious by default |
| Reframe speed | decoy framings discarded fast |
| Wide exposure | varied inputs resist echo-chamber capture |

**Watchpoints**
- *FOMO as a lifestyle:* scarcity framing blends into normal enthusiasm, making it hard to see as
  a *technique*.
- *Reframe-as-escape:* positive reframing deployed to avoid a needed hard look — "it'll be fine"
  as verification avoidance.
- *Stimulation-loop capture:* engagement loops exploit the novelty budget; each hit trains the
  algorithm and the operator.

**Countermeasures** → `skill.bias.availability` (vivid ≠ representative — wait for it to feel
normal before deciding), `skill.tactic.emotive-framing` (name the hype),
`skill.sift.stop` (the accuracy prompt before the share).

*Disconfirming observation:* an option you passed up *without* FOMO getting louder.

## Cross-type synthesis

Reading the 9 guides side by side, three motivational clusters map onto the same tactic families as
the MBTI synthesis:

1. **Need-and-obligation surfaces** (2, 9, and 1's moral licensing): manufactured need,
   reciprocity inversion, guilt+urgency stacks, agenda adoption. Countermeasure weight on the
   pause and on naming the move.
2. **Recognition-and-urgency surfaces** (3, 7, 8, 4): flattery with an invoice, false windows,
   justice and recognition baiting, FOMO. Weight on verifying before amplifying and naming the
   bait.
3. **Vigilance-capture surfaces** (5, 6): fascination, pre-digestion, counter-phobic approach,
   reassurance loops. Weight on origin-tracing and independent coverage.

**The honest bottom line:** none of this is predictive of *you* from a single number. It is a map
of where each motivation, once engaged, tends to switch the guard off — and the same verification
toolkit works for all of them. The observation log decides which pages apply to you
([self-report limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5248555/)).
