# SOVEREIGN // AEGIS — Positioning & Community Outreach

**Audience:** inoculation / prebunking researchers, DISARM & FIMI analysts, serious-games and
media-literacy practitioners. **Prepared:** 2026-08-21.

---

## One line

SOVEREIGN // AEGIS is an offline-first, browser-native training suite that turns inoculation
science and the DISARM framework into a measured, replayable practice loop — and then measures
whether the skill **transfers** to material the learner has never practiced.

## What it is

Four pillars in a single instrument: **Cognitive Fortification** (SIFT labs, fallacy & bias
taxonomy, rhetorical steel-manning), **Real-Time Protection** (the VERDAD epistemic audit),
**Signal & OSINT Forensics** (ELA, 2D FFT, STFT spectrogram, C2PA manifest parsing), and
**Collective Defense** (the InfoWar network simulator with a DISARM-mapped after-action review).

The practice surface is games: a Fallacy Gauntlet, a 60-Second Triage, prebunking scenarios, a
predict-then-measure forensics drill, an SM-2 Memory Vault, and an InfoWar wargame whose
post-mortem grades against the **genuine DISARM Blue countermeasure framework**. Zero build
step, zero backend, zero telemetry — it runs from a static host or a single file, and all data
stays on-device.

## Why it speaks your language

- **Inoculation, not debunking.** AEGIS implements *active, technique-based* inoculation: the
  six manipulation techniques from *Bad News* (impersonation, emotion, polarization, conspiracy,
  trolling, discrediting), Cranky Uncle's FLICC fallacy categories, and weakened-dose exposure
  inside the simulators. Prebunk-first is the default posture, not an add-on.
- **DISARM interop.** The app teaches Red TTPs as a shared vocabulary, cites FIMI as the EU
  cousin, and its AAR surfaces stage-matched **Blue countermeasures (C-codes, CC-BY-SA-4.0)** —
  so the post-game grade is earned against the real framework, not a hand-written match.
- **Provenance without overclaiming.** C2PA manifests are *parsed, never "verified"* in-browser;
  the app deep-links to `contentcredentials.org/verify` for real signature adjudication. That
  "measure and label, don't over-claim" discipline is the field's own current best practice.

## The claim worth your attention

The field has proven games: *Cat Park* (≈ +19 points on technique-spotting, reduced
self-reported sharing), *Harmony Square* (RCT, n=681), *Bad News*, *Go Viral!*, *Cranky Uncle*.
What the field does not yet share is a **measurement spine across them**.

AEGIS records every attempt in an append-only log, estimates per-skill mastery with a Bayesian
estimator where *confidence means quantity of evidence, not self-report*, tracks calibration
with a Brier score, logs chosen distractors into a confusion matrix, and — the differentiator —
**holds out unseen items to estimate transfer**. "You practiced these 40 items" and "your held-out
spotting moved from 55% to 71%, CI [..]" are different claims. Only the second is evidence of
inoculation, and AEGIS reports the second honestly.

## Honest-by-construction (why you can cite it)

- Measurements ship with their limitations; no verdict is invented where the instrument cannot
  support one.
- Claimed confidence is quarantined from mastery — saying "sure" cannot inflate the estimate.
- The triage game scores a correct "continue" exactly equal to a correct "stop" (no cynicism bonus).
- In the docs, every recommendation is labelled *proven* versus *design judgement*, with sources.

## Ways to engage

- **Run a transfer study on it.** The app already computes baseline-vs-current on held-out items
  with honest confidence intervals. Pre-register a protocol and AEGIS becomes the instrument —
  no reimplementation, no participant data leaving the device.
- **Contribute taxonomy or stimuli.** DISARM Blue is already adopted; add your inoculation
  stimuli, FLICC/FIMI mappings, or MISP case-pack export as plain JSON.
- **Pressure-test the mapping.** We want DISARM practitioners to find where our Red/Blue stage
  mapping is weak before we claim interop more loudly.

**The ask:** a read, a citation, or a short call. We are looking for researchers who want a
ready instrument for a prebunking/inoculation transfer experiment, and practitioners willing to
audit the DISARM and provenance surfaces.
